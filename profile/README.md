GreyBody Games is an independent game studio building focused, atmospheric experiences where physical systems and human decisions tell the story.

We care about tactile interactions, believable spaces, deliberate scope, and technology that serves the player experience rather than overshadowing it.

## Currently building

### Andromeda

**An orbital salvage station work-sim where every object may be evidence.**

Operate a compact salvage facility in orbit. Receive recovered cargo, inspect and scan it, reconcile what you find with what the manifest claims, and decide what should be stored, quarantined, sold, reported, dismantled, or quietly forgotten.

> Inspect, scan, classify, and store the things space sends back.

[Visit the Andromeda website](https://andromeda.greybodygames.com/) · [Follow development at GreyBody Games](https://greybodygames.com/)

## How we build

- Focused games with a strong mechanical and emotional core
- Systems that create stories through player decisions
- Believable industrial spaces and physical workflows
- Native Unreal Engine gameplay architecture
- Procedural tools that improve consistency without flattening authorship
- Documentation and repeatable pipelines that keep ambitious work maintainable
